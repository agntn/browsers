::provider-facts{name="cloudflare"}
::

Cloudflare's Browser Run has a REST route for nearly everything: `/markdown`, `/content`, `/screenshot`, `/pdf`, `/links`, `/json` for extract, `/accessibilityTree`, and `/crawl` for jobs. No session needed for any of them. It covers more operations than any other provider here.

It wants two values: `CF_API_TOKEN` and `CF_ACCOUNT_ID`. `CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN` and `CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID` work too.

Ask for more than one output and it renders once: `formats: ["markdown", "html"]`, or `screenshot: true`, go through `/snapshot` and come back together. A scrape also fills `title` and `statusCode`.

The markdown in the recording has the words of example.com glued together, `Thisdomainisforuse…`. Jina Reader spaced the same page out letter by letter, so the page is doing something odd with its markup and two converters read it two ways. Other pages come out normal.

`browser: "kitesurf"` switches to Cloudflare's Kitesurf engine. Without it you're on Chromium.
