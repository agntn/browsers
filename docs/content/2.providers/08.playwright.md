---
title: Playwright
description: Chromium on your own machine. No key and no account and no bill. Scrape and PDFs and links and crawl locally
---

::provider-facts{name="playwright"}
::

Playwright is the one that runs here. It launches the Chrome, Chromium or Edge already on your `PATH`, or the Chromium that `npx playwright-core install chromium` downloads, and never needs a key. It's last in the registry, so it's what you get when nothing else is configured.

`scrape()` returns everything at once: `title`, `html`, the page's `innerText` as `text`, and every link. The tool picks the text, which is why its answer in the recording is the plain words of the page.

Sessions live in your process. A session opened by one CLI command is gone when that command exits, so `browsers session create -p playwright` is mostly a party trick. In code, `createSession()` keeps the browser until `releaseSession()`.

No CDP URL, since the browser is yours already. And no bill, which is nice.
